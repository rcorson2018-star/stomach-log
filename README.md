# Stomach Log

A simple, single-file food-and-symptom diary for spotting what's upsetting your stomach.

Log what you eat, mark how your stomach feels on a Good / Meh / Bad scale (or an exact number out of 10), and let the built-in "possible suspects" panel surface foods that show up more often on your worse-feeling entries.

## Features

- **Quick add** — one-tap logging for foods you eat often (customizable list)
- **Good / Meh / Bad or 0–10** severity scale, editable after the fact
- **Possible suspects** — a rough client-side correlation between foods/symptoms and severity
- **CSV export** to share with a doctor
- All data stays in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere

## Usage

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or serve it statically (e.g. GitHub Pages).
